About Tech Education Services

How six citizen founders built Francistown's first affordable, structured one-stop ICT training and repair hub — and where we're headed next.

Our Story

How We Came About

Tech Education Services (Pty) Ltd was born from a simple observation in Francistown: youth wanted digital skills, but Gaborone-based training was too expensive and too far. Repair shops were informal and unreliable. No one offered both — until we did.

A group survey of 60 Francistown youths found that 78% wanted structured ICT training, citing cost and access as their biggest barriers. Six students from Botswana Accountancy College studying BSc Business Intelligence and Data Analytics — skilled in Excel, Word, Power BI, Python, SQL, and data science — came together to fill that gap.

Registered with CIPA as a Private Company (Pty) Ltd, we combined Learn • Repair • Sustain into one hub: students graduate from tutorials, sit certification exams, buy devices from our store, and many become the technicians who serve the next customer. It's a virtuous cycle designed for employment and community impact.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

2024–2025

The Idea Takes Shape

Founders conduct market research in Francistown. Survey confirms 78% youth interest. Business model drafted combining training, repairs, and parts sales.

Early 2026

Formal Registration & Funding

CIPA incorporation complete. Blended loan proposal (P105,390) prepared for CEDA, YDF, and commercial banks. Premises identified in central Francistown.

Mid 2026 — Launch

Hub Opens & First Cohorts

Training room and workshop operational. First cohort discount (20%), school partnerships, and Google My Business launch. Target: 200+ students Year 1.

Year 2

Scale & Partnerships

Corporate B2B contracts, expanded repair throughput, P510,000 net profit target. Technicians and admin staff hired.

Year 3

Gaborone Expansion

Second branch opens. Revenues exceed P2.1M. Leading citizen-owned ICT centre in Botswana.

Year 5

BQA Accreditation

Formal qualification delivery. Become the national benchmark for affordable, quality ICT vocational training.
